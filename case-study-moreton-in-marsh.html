Spitfire Homes Ellenbrook, Moreton-in-Marsh

Air source heat pumps and full plumbing across an initial 155 Cotswold homes.

We delivered the plumbing and heating package for Spitfire Homes' Ellenbrook development in Moreton-in-Marsh, a collection of high-quality homes blending traditional Cotswold architecture with modern, energy-efficient living.

Our works

  • Air source heat pumps across the development
  • Heating systems combining underfloor heating and radiators
  • All hot and cold water services
  • Sanitaryware and sustainable plumbing solutions

Across an initial 155 plots, the renewable technology we installed supports comfortable, low-carbon living in homes designed to be future-proofed.

Working alongside the wider construction team, we delivered reliable, high-specification installations that reflect the quality, craftsmanship and sustainability standards of the Ellenbrook development.
