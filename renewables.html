Government funding

Up to £7,500 off your installation

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme is the UK government's flagship grant for replacing fossil fuel heating. As an MCS-certified installer, GW Renewables can support your BUS grant application, helping you access funding to reduce your installation cost.

  • Property must be in England or Wales
  • Must be replacing a fossil fuel heating system
  • Installer must be MCS-certified (GW Renewables is)
  • Grant is applied to reduce your final installation cost
