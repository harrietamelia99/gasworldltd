Meet the team

Leadership

Meet the directors

Adam Lane
Managing Director

Adam grew up around the business his parents, John and Angela Lane, founded in 1981, and officially joined in 2003 after completing his accountancy degree. Over more than 20 years he worked across every part of the business, helping it grow into one of the largest heating and renewables contractors in the South West. Adam took over as Managing Director in 2023 and keeps strong family values at the heart of the company, with a focus on reliable, high-quality service, trusted client relationships and supporting the next generation of engineers. Outside of work, he enjoys time with his wife and two children, travelling and playing golf.

Mike Stephens
Head of Production

Mike has over 13 years' experience with GasWorld, progressing from engineer to site supervisor, contract manager and now Head of Production. He directs the delivery of all active new build developments from start to completion, working closely with the contracts management team to keep programmes on track and maintain high standards of workmanship across every site. Mike also supports new business opportunities, client relationships and ongoing improvements in quality and health and safety.

Phil Moore
Commercial Director

Phil leads GasWorld's commercial strategy, driving growth across the company's commercial, new build, and developer client base across the South West.

Rob Eason
Estimating & Design Director

Rob joined GasWorld in 2008 and produces estimates from tender drawings through to contract negotiation, securing projects before handing them over to the contracts team. He began his career in 1981 designing public health systems for a consulting engineer, then spent five years as a Mechanical & Electrical Co-ordinator for a construction company, so he has seen installations from drawing board to completion, from both the main contractor and subcontractor side.

WF
Wayne Fry
Social Housing Operations Director

Wayne oversees the day-to-day delivery of GasWorld's social housing contracts, managing engineer teams, programme compliance, and tenant satisfaction.

Dave Ramplin

Ben Chick

Ben manages GasWorld's financial operations, ensuring the business maintains the strong foundations needed to deliver on its growth ambitions.

The wider team

Engineers, estimators & support

Behind our directors is a team of skilled engineers, project managers, estimators, customer care staff and apprentices.

Samantha Curnock
Customer Care Manager

Samantha oversees private customer service and repairs, alongside new-build aftercare, making sure remedial works are delivered within agreed timescales. She leads a small team of office administrators and field engineers, working closely with manufacturers, suppliers and developers to resolve issues quickly and effectively.

Stephen Bacon
Procurement Manager

Stephen manages GasWorld's procurement, sourcing the materials, equipment and products our teams rely on. Working closely with suppliers, manufacturers and our contracts team, he makes sure everything arrives on time, to specification and at the right price, keeping projects running smoothly from start to finish.
